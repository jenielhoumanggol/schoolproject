Time Management Techniques



Practical methods to boost productivity

Below are time-management techniques with their descriptions.

Technique Description
Pomodoro Technique Work for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break.
Time Blocking Assign specific time blocks for different tasks.
Eisenhower Matrix Sort tasks by urgency & importance.
To-Do Lists List tasks clearly and break large tasks into steps.
Avoid Multitasking Focus on one task at a time for better results.
SMART Goals Goals must be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.

Quick Tips

  • Plan tasks the night before
  • Avoid multitasking
  • Focus on one task at a time


Eisenhower Matrix

A decision-making tool that organizes tasks into four categories:

Do First: Important & Urgent
Schedule: Important but Not Urgent
Delegate: Not Important but Urgent
Eliminate: Not Important & Not Urgent
Student Example:
Exams tomorrow → Do First
Reviewing notes → Schedule


To-Do List

  • Write all tasks
  • Break large tasks into steps
  • Check completed tasks
