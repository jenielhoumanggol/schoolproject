Time Management Techniques



Practical methods to boost productivity

Below are time-management techniques with their descriptions.

Technique
Description
Pomodoro Technique
Work for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break.
Time Blocking
Assign specific time blocks for different tasks.
Eisenhower Matrix
Sort tasks by urgency & importance to prioritize properly.
To-Do Lists
List tasks clearly and break large tasks into steps.
Avoid Multitasking
Focus on one task at a time for better results.
SMART Goals
Goals must be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.

Eisenhower Matrix

A decision-making tool that organizes tasks into four categories:

Do First
Important & Urgent
(Immediate tasks)
Schedule
Important but Not Urgent
(Plan these tasks)
Delegate
Not Important but Urgent
(Let others handle)
Eliminate
Not Important & Not Urgent
(Remove distractions)

To-Do List

A simple, effective tool for organizing tasks.

  • Write all tasks for the day or week.
  • Break large tasks into smaller steps.
  • Check or strike out completed tasks.
  • Prioritize tasks from highest to lowest importance.

Time Blocks

Divide your day into dedicated blocks for specific tasks.

Example:

8:00–10:00 AM — Study Science
10:00–11:00 AM — Break / Chores
11:00–1:00 PM — Homework / Projects
2:00–4:00 PM — Review / Reading
